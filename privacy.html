Privacy policy

Who is responsible

Airoo is an app by [NOMBRE Y APELLIDOS] (NIF [DNI CON LETRA]). For anything about your data, write to airoapp@proton.me.

What data we use and why

Airoo has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and does not sell or share data with anyone for other purposes.

Location. If you allow it, the app uses your phone's position; otherwise, the place you search for. It is only used to get the weather and radar for that place. Coordinates are rounded (to ~1 km) and sent to our server, and from there to Open-Meteo for the forecast. They are not linked to any data about you.

What your phone keeps. Your settings, saved places, the alerts you want and the last forecast stay on your phone. Notifications are prepared on the phone itself.

Airoo+ purchases. Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) charge them; we never see your name or payment details. To know whether you have Airoo+ we use RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous identifier created by the app and your store purchase history.

Updates. App updates are delivered by Expo (EAS Update), which receives technical data about the phone (system, app version).

Error reports. If something breaks, the app sends Sentry an anonymous report: what failed and where, the app version, the system and the language. Never your location or anything that identifies you. You can turn it off in Settings → Privacy.

Why we can use them

To provide the service you ask for (the weather for your place and, if you subscribe, Airoo+): Article 6(1)(b) of the General Data Protection Regulation. Phone location only if you allow it in the system; you can revoke it at any time in your phone's settings. Error reports, on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the app working (Article 6(1)(f)); you can turn them off in Settings.

Who it is shared with

Only with the services that make the app work, and only for that: Supabase (our server), Open-Meteo (the forecast), RevenueCat (subscriptions), Apple and Google (purchases and the store), Expo (updates) and Sentry (error reports). Some are outside the European Union (in the United States); in that case, with the safeguards the law requires (standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework).

How long

What is on your phone, until you delete it or uninstall the app. Our server keeps weather responses per rounded place, with nothing that identifies you, for one day at most. Purchase records, as long as the stores and the law (e.g. tax law) require.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to or port your data by writing to airoapp@proton.me. If you think we have not handled it properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or your local authority.

Children

Airoo is not intended for children under 14 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will say so in the app and the date above will change.
